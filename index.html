About

K M Wahiduzzaman Fahad is a data analyst working in AI and automation, and a Lecturer of Business Analytics at BRAC University, where he teaches predictive analytics and machine learning, Python for business analytics, and management information systems. Driven by a wish to see a more secure world, his research sits at the intersection of machine learning, explainable AI and cybersecurity. He also studies the socially responsible investment intention–behavior gap and the cost of deploying large language models in Bangladesh.

Before entering academia, he spent several years in industry building data systems: automating ETL pipelines into Snowflake and ERP systems as a Data System Analyst at EquipmentShare (USA), leading marketing analytics and demand forecasting for a retail group in Bangladesh, and founding a digital transformation agency that worked with the National Housing Authority of Bangladesh. He holds an M.S. in Business Analytics from Texas A&M University-Commerce (CGPA 3.9) and a BBA in Finance from BRAC University (CGPA 3.57).
